## The standing problem (this is the actual job now)
The owner's verdict: **engineering is strong; the visual design is NOT yet "best on the internet."**
Pages still skew information-dense and "feel off." The owner reacts to *feel*, not feature lists.
Hard-won lessons from this session:
- **Bias to SUBTRACTION and ONE dominant format per page.** The recurring failure was stacking
  modules that re-slice the same data (Contracts had six). Cut/relocate before composing.
- **Do NOT bolt "ledes"/hero strips onto a bloated page** — the owner called that "lipstick."
- **Propose a direction, then build.** Don't stamp a template across pages.
- The owner has strong, specific taste — calibrate on ONE page, get reaction, then roll out.

## Chosen workflow going forward = tight render-loop
Can't reach the owner's Chrome from the sandbox. Instead: drive the headless real app via the harness,
capture **component-level / zoomed / interaction-state** screenshots (extend `capture.js` beyond
full-page shots as needed), do focused per-page redesign passes, verify against the invariant gate,
commit small. **Start with Home and Power** (highest traffic), then the Stats cluster.

## Remaining (non-design)
- **Performance loop:** kill the global `*` transition rule, debounce history-merge re-renders, keep
  the player DB off the localStorage quota cliff — best verified on the deployed Pages site.
- **Mobile/a11y finish:** stop long team-name truncation (wrap), keyboard-reach every inline
  `.gm-link`, alt-text audit.

## How to verify any change
`node --check` the extracted script → `node dev/audit/capture.js --tabs=<changed>` (lean) → look at
1–2 crops → atomic commit (`type(scope): desc`) → push to `claude/vibrant-ptolemy-9t3alm`. One full
both-mode regression before declaring a batch done. Real-data sign-off happens on the live Pages site
(live APIs are blocked in the sandbox).
