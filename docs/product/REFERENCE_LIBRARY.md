## Reference evaluation questions

Every composition study must answer all four before it advances:

1. Can a fantasy manager identify the familiar fantasy interaction without instructions?
2. Is the league-specific layer impossible to confuse with a generic platform?
3. Does imagery or data visualization carry meaning rather than merely decorate a card?
4. Can the first task be completed on a 390px screen without shrinking a desktop table?

## Reference capture policy

External product captures used in a blueprint packet must record the product, page, capture
date, viewport, and the exact hierarchy or interaction being studied. They are review-only
research artifacts and are not shipped. Any future internet research uses current primary
product surfaces and must not introduce unlicensed images into the app.
